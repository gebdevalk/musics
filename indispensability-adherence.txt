================================================================================
indispensability-adherence -- possibilities for shifting Barlow
indispensability weights via an adherence factor (-1.0 .. +1.0)
================================================================================

Working notes from a design conversation about algo.indisp.indispensability's
existing tilt-probabilities fn (softmax over ranks, adherence as temperature)
and the "area of equal values" problem it has at adherence=0 (every exponent
collapses to exp(0)=1, giving every pulse identical probability). Six distinct
ways to let adherence reshape the weights, none of which have this collapse,
each changing a genuinely different aspect of the rhythm. Not all implemented
yet -- this is the survey, not the build log.

  Name                    | Technical                                        | Musical effect
  ------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tilt / contrast         | Softmax over ranks, adherence as inverse-         | Controls how CONFIDENTLY the meter asserts itself. High: the
                          | temperature -- sharper adherence pushes           | downbeat and strong beats are unmistakable, everything else
                          | probability mass toward higher ranks. Fixed for   | recedes -- solid, grounded, "on the grid." Near the middle: the
                          | the zero-collapse by adding a tiny, irrational,   | sense of strong-vs-weak blurs, the music floats, losing its
                          | position-based term INSIDE the exponent (not      | metric footing without yet syncopating. Negative: the hierarchy
                          | scaled by adherence itself), so at adherence=0    | flips outright -- weak, off-beat positions become the ones that
                          | only that term survives -- still strictly        | jump out, an inverted groove that consistently lands where you
                          | increasing in position, never flat.               | don't expect it.
  ------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------
  Discrete switch         | No blending at all -- sign of adherence selects   | A hard cut between two fixed characters, no in-between: "straight"
                          | between the ranks as-is or their exact reversal   | (right on the beat) or "flipped" (landing squarely on the weak
                          | (both already tie-free permutations on their      | positions instead). No drift, no blur -- a sudden character
                          | own); magnitude can still separately drive tilt   | change, like a section that abruptly starts playing AGAINST the
                          | sharpness via tilt-probabilities. Trivially       | groove rather than gradually working its way there. Good for a
                          | tie-free since nothing is ever interpolated.      | structural contrast (verse straight, chorus flipped), not an
                          |                                                    | ongoing drift.
  ------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rotation / phase shift  | Cyclically rotate the rank vector by some offset  | The groove's own shape stays exactly the same, but WHERE "the
                          | (algo.common.rotate) -- displaces which pulse     | one" lands moves. Like a drummer playing an identical pattern
                          | holds which rank without changing the rank        | but starting it from a different point in the bar -- what used
                          | distribution's own shape at all.                  | to feel like beat 1 now carries beat 3's weight. The rhythm's
                          |                                                    | personality is unchanged; only its relationship to the barline
                          |                                                    | is displaced -- "playing behind/ahead of the beat."
  ------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------
  Local swap /            | Randomly swap ranks between ADJACENT pulses with  | Small, unpredictable hiccups scattered through the bar rather
  perturbation            | probability tied to adherence -- same mutation    | than one consistent shift: individual notes here and there
                          | skeleton algo.rhythmic.transform/                 | landing slightly off from where the meter would put them,
                          | emi-style-variation already uses, applied to      | randomly, not systematically. Sounds like human imprecision, or
                          | rank order instead of a binary grid. Stochastic,   | a stutter/glitch quality -- looseness and unpredictability in
                          | not deterministic -- every run differs.           | isolated spots, rather than a single global character.
  ------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------
  Power-law reshaping     | Raise (normalized) ranks to an exponent > 0 --    | Keeps strong beats strong and weak beats weak (never reorders
                          | always order-preserving, so it changes            | anything), but changes how steeply importance falls off between
                          | contrast/curvature of the distribution without    | them. Sharpened: only the single strongest beat really matters,
                          | ever reordering or tying anything.                | everything else is nearly equally unimportant -- a stark,
                          |                                                    | isolated accent against a flat backdrop. Gentled: importance
                          |                                                    | spreads more evenly across many beats -- a smooth gradient from
                          |                                                    | strong down to weak, nothing feeling truly negligible.
  ------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------
  Interval-based          | Apply a smoothing/gain transform to the           | Changes how SMOOTHLY the feeling of emphasis travels from one
  reshaping               | DIFFERENCES between consecutive ranks, then       | beat to the next, rather than how strong any single beat is.
                          | reconstruct -- same skeleton algo.common.zfilter/ | Smoothed: strong-to-weak transitions feel blended and connected,
                          | smooth-intervals/interval-gain already use.       | like a legato phrasing of accents. Exaggerated: the jumps
                          |                                                    | between strong and weak feel abrupt and jagged -- sudden accent
                          |                                                    | contrasts rather than a continuous contour.

================================================================================
Context: base voice + counter voice
================================================================================

Motivating use case: a BASE voice that adheres strictly to the meter (fixed,
e.g. adherence=1.0, or just the raw indispensability ranks played straight),
and a COUNTER voice whose OWN adherence can vary independently -- to become
more or less syncopic relative to the base. Tilt/contrast and discrete switch
are the two candidates that read most directly as "how syncopic is THIS
voice right now" -- rotation and local-swap are closer to "where/how loosely
does this voice sit," genuinely different musical questions, not mutually
exclusive with the first two.

Open question, not yet decided: whether the counter voice's adherence should
be a plain function argument (composer supplies a value per call) or wired
the way logistic-algo/henon-algo/lorenz-algo's own parameters got wired
earlier the same session (core.wall/context-params-pre-step-fn) -- sampled
live from a context envelope, so syncopation level can ramp during an actual
performance, authored as a !adherence: curve in the material itself.
